# Turning DestaHealth ATS Into a Product

## Where we are today

We built DestaHealth ATS to run our own recruiting. It works: our pipeline, our clients, our
candidates, all in one place. But it only serves one company — us. Every hour we put into it
improves our own operation and nothing else.

Other healthcare staffing firms have exactly the problem we solved. Most are still running this work on
the spreadsheets we abandoned.

## What we are proposing

Make the same system serve many staffing firms at once, each in its own private space.

A firm signs up, gets their own workspace, and sees only their own candidates and clients.
They cannot see ours. We cannot see theirs. The separation is enforced by the database itself
rather than by each screen remembering to ask — and every change we make is checked against it
automatically, before it can go live.

---

## What a firm gets

This is what we would actually be selling. All of it exists today and we use it daily.

**One place for the whole pipeline.** Every candidate moves through the same thirteen stages, from
first contact to first day on the job. Anyone can see where every candidate is, right now, without
asking. No more "which spreadsheet is the current one".

**Candidate-to-client fit, scored automatically.** The system knows what each client needs —
state, credential, population, setting, licence — and scores every candidate against it out of
100. A recruiter opens a client and sees who fits, ranked, instead of scanning a list by memory.

**A portal for their clients.** Their client sees the candidates submitted to them, in their own
private view, and can post new roles they need filled. That replaces the email chain that
currently loses attachments and buries decisions.

**Sourcing before the pipeline.** Leads are tracked from first outreach through follow-ups to the
moment they become a real candidate — so the work that happens *before* someone enters the
pipeline stops being invisible.

**Eleven reports, already built.** Executive summary, pipeline funnel, time-in-stage, team
performance, source ROI, client capacity and portfolio, compliance, and more. These answer the
questions an owner actually asks: where are we losing people, which sources are worth the money,
who on my team is converting.

**AI that does the boring parts.** Resumes are read and turned into a candidate record instead of
being retyped. A daily brief summarises what needs attention; a weekly one summarises the week. We
are not tied to one AI vendor, so we are not exposed to one company's pricing.

**Licence and credential tracking.** Healthcare recruiting lives or dies on this. Expiries and
verification states are tracked and surfaced before they become a problem.

**Roles, so people see what they should.** Six levels from Owner down to Associate. A screener
does not need — and does not get — the owner's view.

**Nothing to install.** It runs in a browser. No server in their office, no IT person, no upgrades
to schedule. When we improve something, they have it the next day.

## How a firm would run it

1. **They sign up** and get their own empty workspace.
2. **They invite their recruiters** by email and give each one a role.
3. **They add their clients** and what each client is looking for.
4. **They work.** Add candidates, move them through stages, submit to clients, track licences.
5. **Their clients get a portal link** and can see submissions without anyone emailing a
   spreadsheet.
6. **They pay us monthly.** If they stop, we switch the workspace off — and can switch it back on
   with their information untouched.

Day to day, nobody has to think about the fact that other firms are using the same system. That
is the whole point.

## What we get

**Income that does not depend only on our own placements.** Today we earn when we place someone.
As a product, we also earn every month from every firm using it, whether or not we place anyone
that month. That is steadier money, and it grows without us hiring more recruiters.

**Each new customer costs us almost nothing.** No new system, no new machine, no separate
installation. Adding the tenth firm takes about as much effort as adding the second, and every
improvement we make lands for all of them at once.

**We already paid for the hard part.** The system exists and is in daily use. This is opening it
up, not starting over.

**Our customers pay for improvements we wanted anyway.** Every feature a paying firm asks for is
one our own recruiters get too. Their money funds our own tool getting better.

**We learn what the market wants.** Ten firms using this tells us more about where healthcare
staffing is going than we can learn from our own desk alone.

**The tool becomes an asset.** A staffing company with a product other staffing firms pay for is
worth more than a staffing company with a good internal tool. That matters whenever anyone
is valuing this business.

---

## Why a firm would buy from us

**Because we are one of them.** This was not built by software people guessing at recruiting. It
was built by a healthcare staffing firm to run its own desk, and every screen exists because
somebody here needed it. That is a sales advantage no general-purpose recruiting tool has, and it
is the first thing to say in any pitch.

**Because their data is safer here than in a spreadsheet.** Healthcare recruiting means holding
real people's licences, contact details and history. A spreadsheet emailed around has no idea who
opened it. Here, every workspace is separated at the database level, every change is recorded, and
who can see what is a setting rather than a habit. That is what the regulations expect, and it is
what a firm will ask about before they sign.

---

## Where it stands

The separation is built and tested — two firms can already use the system side by side without
seeing each other, and that is re-proven automatically every time we change anything.

The system is also now packaged so it can run on whatever hosting we choose, and we have run it
end to end to confirm that works.

Three things remain before we can sell it: **choosing where it runs** and putting it there,
**moving our existing records across**, and **adding sign-up and billing**. The first is a decision
more than a job. The second is the biggest piece of work. The third is what turns it from something
a firm could use into something they can buy.
