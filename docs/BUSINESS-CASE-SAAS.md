# Turning DestaHealth ATS Into a Product

**A proposal for Biruh** · August 2026

---

## Where we are today

We built DestaHealth ATS to run our own recruiting. It works: our pipeline, our clients, our
candidates, all in one place. But it only serves one company — us. Every hour we put into it
improves our own operation and nothing else.

Other staffing agencies have exactly the problem we solved. They are running the same work on
spreadsheets we abandoned.

## What we are proposing

Make the same system serve many agencies at once, each in its own private space.

An agency signs up, gets their own workspace, and sees only their own candidates and clients.
They cannot see ours. We cannot see theirs. Nobody has to be told to be careful — the system
simply cannot show one agency another's information.

## Why this is worth doing

**Our income stops depending only on our own placements.** Today we earn when we place someone.
As a product, we also earn every month from every agency using it, whether or not we place
anyone that month. That is steadier money, and it grows without us hiring more recruiters.

**Each new customer costs us almost nothing.** We do not build a new system, buy a new machine,
or run a separate installation for each one. Adding the tenth agency takes about as much effort
as adding the second. Every improvement we make lands for all of them at once.

**We already paid for the hard part.** The system exists. The work here is opening it up, not
starting over.

**Our clients' data is safer, not less safe.** Healthcare recruiting means holding real people's
licences, contact details and history. Separating each agency completely is now built into how
the system works rather than left to whoever is being careful that day. That is what the
regulations expect of us, and it is what an agency will ask about before they sign.

**A customer who stops paying can be switched off immediately** — and switched back on just as
easily, with their information untouched.

## What it changes for us

We stop being a staffing company with a good internal tool, and become a staffing company that
also sells one. The tool becomes an asset with its own value, not just a cost we carry.

## Where it stands

The separation is built and tested. Two agencies can already use the system side by side without
seeing each other. What remains before we can sell it: putting it online properly, moving our
existing records across, and adding sign-up and billing.

## What we need to decide

1. **Do we want to sell this, or keep it internal?** Everything below depends on this answer.
2. **Who is the first customer?** One friendly agency teaches us more than six months of guessing.
3. **What do we charge, and per what** — per recruiter, per placement, or a flat monthly fee?

None of these are technical questions. They are the ones only you can answer.
